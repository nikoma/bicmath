Tie-breaking policy (documented and returned by BicMath): each exact share is floored to minor units; the remaining minor units are distributed one at a time to recipients ordered by descending fractional remainder, with ties broken by ascending index. This largest-remainder rule is deterministic and makes the shares sum exactly to the original total. Here all three remainders tie, so recipient 1 receives the single cent.

Method used: the descriptor documents sf(x) = P(X > x) = 0.5 · erfc((x − mean) / (sd · √2)), computed from erfc directly — never as 1 − cdf — so large positive x keeps relative accuracy.

9. Units and dimensions

ConversionResultExactness / notes
Absolute temperature: 0 °C → K 273.15 K exact; affine offset applied
Temperature increase: 10 °C → K difference 10 K exact; assumption temperature_delta
5 kg → meters error incompatible_units
{"code": "incompatible_units", "message": "cannot convert from kilogram (kg) to meter (m)"}

Why absolute and delta must be distinguished: the Celsius→Kelvin conversion is affine — it adds 273.15 to an absolute temperature. A temperature increase is a pure scale conversion (factor 1 for °C→K), so applying the offset would wrongly turn a 10 °C increase into 283.15 K. BicMath exposes units.temperature_difference for the delta case and records the temperature_delta assumption.
